# Vision

India's best movement knowledge should not remain trapped inside one athlete, one coach, or one training centre.

Motion Twin AI converts skilled human movement into measurable, comparable, and teachable intelligence. The system captures motion through wearables and video, reconstructs it in 3D, links movement to outcomes, and produces personalized guidance for athletes.

The long-term asset is an outcome-grounded Human Performance Foundation Model trained on:

- motion
- physiology
- context
- intent
- performance outcome
- expert coaching annotations

The watches and phone are the affordable data-collection edge. The durable company is the intelligence and data infrastructure behind them.
