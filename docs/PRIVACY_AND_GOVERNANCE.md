# Privacy and Governance

Human movement data can identify a person and reveal health, fatigue, injury, and performance characteristics. Treat it as sensitive data.

## Required controls
- explicit informed consent
- separate consent for training feedback, research, and model training
- pseudonymous athlete IDs
- encryption in transit and at rest
- role-based coach and researcher access
- retention and deletion controls
- no facial recognition by default
- no undisclosed model training
- human review for high-stakes recommendations

## Defence profile
- no dependency on public cloud
- offline and on-premise operation
- institution-controlled keys
- export restrictions and audit logs
- separation of sports and operational datasets
