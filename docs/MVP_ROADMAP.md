# MVP Roadmap

## Phase 0: feasibility
- record raw IMU from one Galaxy Watch
- verify sample-rate stability and background behavior
- render a 3D wrist-orientation trail

## Phase 1: dual-watch capture
- left/right pairing
- synchronized countdown and calibration gesture
- local buffering and phone transfer
- timing-error measurement

## Phase 2: archery session app
- phone tripod capture
- shot detection
- manual result entry
- attempt replay and comparison

## Phase 3: cloud reconstruction
- 2D/3D pose pipeline
- wrist IMU fusion
- phase segmentation
- best-vs-current overlays

## Phase 4: pilot
- 5 elite or expert participants
- 15 developing athletes
- 6 to 8 weeks
- coach annotations
- objective shot-outcome comparison

## Phase 5: platformization
- academy dashboard
- multi-sport adapters
- secure deployment mode
- model and dataset governance
